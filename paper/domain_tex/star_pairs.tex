Here, choice items are pairs of movie stars from a set of four stars: Tom Hanks, Scarlett Johansson, Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie.
We propose a similarity relation where two star pairs are similar if they have a star in common.
The only star pairs that are not similar are $c$ and $d$.
{}

Knowing only who is starring, which one of these new films would you choose to see?

	\item Tom Hanks and Scarlett Johansson
	\item Scarlett Johansson and Brad Pitt
	\item Tom Hanks and Brad Pitt
	\item Scarlett Johansson and Angelina Jolie
	\item Tom Hanks and Angelina Jolie
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
