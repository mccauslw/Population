This domain involves comparisons of the probabilities of possible future events.
Logically, the probability of event $e$ must be at least as great as the probability of $a$, which must in turn be at least as great as the probability of $d$; also, the probability of $b$ must be at least as great as the probability of $c$.
We propose a dominance relation based on these objective probability rankings.
The dominance relation is somewhat unconventional, as most experimental designs in the literature on attraction effects feature numerical dominance relations.
