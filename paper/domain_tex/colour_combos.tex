The source for this domain is the website ``The top tens'', page ``Two colors that look good side by side.''
The color combinations here are ranked 1, 4, 5, 13 and 14.
We chose a set of combinations with many colors in common.
We propose a similarity relation where two colour combinations are similar if they have a colour in common.
All pairs of combinations are similar except $a$ and $e$, and $b$ and $d$.
The name for this domain used in data files and documents, ``Colour combos'' is an abbreviation of the title seen by participants, ``Colour combinations''.
